# Resume review checklist

Use this checklist selectively. Do not turn every item into a mandatory section or ask the user to provide details that are already in the resume.

## Relevance and scanability

- Is the target role clear from context or the provided job description?
- Can a reader find the candidate's strongest relevant evidence quickly?
- Does each section earn its space for this application?
- Is the chosen length readable, without arbitrary one-page compression?

## Personal summary

- On Chinese resumes, is the heading exactly `个人综述`?
- Are there at most three concise points?
- Does each point have evidence and connect to the role?
- Are platform, education, skills, portfolio, or language claims specific and accurate?
- Are generic self-praise and unsupported claims removed?

## Experience

- Is the project or business context understandable?
- Are goals/problems, personal actions, deliverables, and outcomes distinguishable?
- Is individual contribution separated from team outcome?
- Are metrics attributable, interpretable, and tied to a period or baseline when relevant?
- If metrics are unavailable, is there other verified evidence of scope or impact?

## Content and accuracy

- Are repeated details, irrelevant coursework, generic evaluations, and unsupported skill labels removed or shortened?
- Are dates, titles, employers, and claims internally consistent?
- Are unknown facts marked for confirmation rather than guessed?
- Are personal information and layout choices consistent with user preference and application requirements?
