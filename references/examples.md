# Examples

These are structural examples only. Replace every bracketed field with verified candidate information. Never copy example claims into a real resume as facts.

## 个人综述（Personal summary）

For a Chinese resume, use the heading `个人综述` exactly. Do not rename it to `个人简介` or `自我评价`.

```text
- [相关经历/平台]，聚焦 [岗位相关领域]；参与/负责 [可核实的项目或成果]。
- 使用 [具体技能/工具] 为 [用户/业务流程] 交付 [产物]，带来 [可核实的结果]。
- [语言/作品集/其他差异点]，证据为 [可核实的证明]。
```

If only one or two points are supported, use one or two. Do not invent a third point to fill the template.

## Experience overview and bullets

```text
[Company / project] | [Role] | [Dates]
Overview: To address [verified problem or goal], worked on [scope/approach]; the team achieved [verified outcome], while I owned [specific contribution].
- [Workstream]: analyzed [evidence/input] and delivered [artifact/decision]; [verified result or scope].
- [Workstream]: implemented [action] with [partners/tools]; changed [measured or observable outcome].
```

When the outcome is a team result, retain the team attribution and state the candidate's role precisely.

## Missing metric

```text
- Reworked [process/artifact] for [known users or cases], reducing [known friction] through [specific change].
  [待补：如有记录，补充处理时长/覆盖范围及统计周期。]
```

Do not estimate a metric unless the user provides a defensible estimate and labels it as such.
